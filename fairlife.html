PRIVACY POLICY
Fair Life - A Creep Games

1. Internet Connection Requirement

Unlike our other titles, Fair Life is a 100% offline game and does not require an internet connection at any time, not even at the start of every login. You can play Fair Life fully disconnected from the internet from the very first launch.

2. Information We Collect

Fair Life does not ask for any personal data.

  • No need to register an account.
  • No need to login to an account.
  • Does not take any data from the Fair Life app, such as player save games, because save games use the local storage of the player's device.
  • No gameplay analytics.
  • Does not take any activity from the application.
  • Does not display third-party advertisements.
  • Does not sell or share any user data.

Every action that makes a difference in the probability of something happening in the game is static code that has many branches for many possibilities.

3. Children's Privacy

Since Fair Life does not collect any personal information, no personal data from children is collected or processed.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated in the future, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

⚠️ Fair Life runs fully offline, even on the very first launch, and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data from your device.
