PRIVACY POLICY
Business Field - A Creep Games
Effective Date and Last Updated: October 28, 2026

1. Internet Connection

Business Field can be played completely offline. All game resources, including the 3D graphics engine, are packaged inside the app, so no internet connection is needed to play.

While you play, the game does not communicate with any server, whether operated by us or by a third party.

2. Information We Collect

Business Field does not ask for any personal data.

  • No need to register an account.
  • No need to login to an account.
  • No gameplay analytics, tracking, or crash reporting.
  • Does not take any activity from the application.
  • Does not display advertisements, and does not use any third-party advertising or analytics service.
  • Does not offer in-app purchases, and does not collect any payment information.
  • Does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, messages, or location.
  • Does not sell or share any user data.

Every event in the game that depends on chance, such as when customers arrive and what they buy, is calculated by code that runs locally on your device.

The store from which you download the app (for example, Google Play) may collect its own information about your download in accordance with its own privacy policy. We do not control that collection.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Business Field stores your preferences, such as the chosen language, graphics quality, FPS display, and screen rotation, in the local storage of your device so that they are remembered the next time you play.

  • This data stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Children's Privacy

Since Business Field does not collect any personal information, no personal data from children is collected or processed.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated in the future, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

⚠️ Business Field works offline. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data from your device.
